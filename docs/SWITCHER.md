# Companion switcher

## Why v2

v1 could freeze on a missing model, treated random male peds as companions, left a second Sweet walking around, and did not restore CJ after death.

## v2 behavior

- Scan radius 22 m
- Named toast: SWEET / SMOKE / RYDER / CESAR / OG LOC / KENDL / THE TRUTH / CATALINA
- Original companion is **hidden**, not destroyed, then shown again on J
- Model load gives up after ~90 frames
- Death while switched restores CJ
- **G** cycles to another companion if two are nearby

## Keys

H become · G next · J CJ · R reset
